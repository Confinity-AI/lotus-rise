Why we started

The mission should shape the technology.

Foundations and nonprofits know their work. Too often, their software does not. Strategy, evaluation and reporting end up spread across systems and rebuilt by hand.

We started Lotus Rise to make the technology fit the mission, so people can spend less time managing systems and more time changing lives.

What we come back to.

Lotus Rise is a public benefit corporation. Our purpose is built into the company: help the social sector amplify its impact with practical, trustworthy technology.

Empowerment, innovation, integrity and community have guided Lotus Rise from the start.
01

Empowerment

Give people useful tools, clear evidence and the final say.

02

Innovation

Use new technology where it solves a real problem. Novelty on its own is not progress.

03

Integrity

Take the long view. Be honest about what the product can do, and make the work easy to check.

04

Community

Build with foundations, nonprofits and evaluators. Share what works so good ideas travel.

Stay close to the people doing the work.

We test Janus against real evaluations and change it when the product does not fit the work. People remain responsible for the final call.
