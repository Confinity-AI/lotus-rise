Build the record as the work happens.

Janus follows the same five steps your evaluation does. Questions, measures, evidence and findings stay linked from setup to review, so the report is the end of the path, not a fresh start.

  1. 01
    Profile

    Describe the program, who it serves and what it hopes to change.

  2. 02
    Design

    Set the evaluation questions and decide how you will measure them.

  3. 03
    Fieldwork

    Gather the evidence and keep it with its source.

  4. 04
    Analysis

    Draw findings that point back to the evidence behind them.

  5. 05
    Deliverables

    Review the report and approve what is ready to share.
