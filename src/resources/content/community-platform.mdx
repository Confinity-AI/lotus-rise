The community platform adds an interactive layer to your ecosystem. It allows users, customers, or your team to gather, share, and engage around your product.

It’s built on Supa Social — a fully functional, self-hostable forum system — and can be adapted to your specific use case.

![Community platform preview](/images/community-01.jpg)

## Flexible by design

The platform can take different roles depending on your needs:

- Internal space for your team
- Public community for your users
- A standalone product or niche network

It’s not locked into a single format — it evolves with your use case.

![Community platform preview](/images/community-02.jpg)

## Built-in social functionality

All core interaction mechanics are already in place:

- Posts and structured content formats
- Comments and discussions
- User profiles and follows
- Notifications and activity

You get a working system, not just a UI.

![Community platform preview](/images/community-03.jpg)

## Connected to your ecosystem

The community integrates naturally with:

- Your product → shared users and context
- Your brand → consistent design and identity
- Your content → discussions around real topics

It becomes part of your product, not an external platform.

## From audience to participation

Instead of relying on external platforms, you create a space where:

- Conversations stay in context
- Users contribute directly
- Community grows around your product

It shifts your presence from broadcasting to participation.
