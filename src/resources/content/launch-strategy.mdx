The launch strategy brings everything together into a coherent presence. It aligns your product, brand, and communication so that when people discover you, everything feels intentional and complete.

It’s not just about launching — it’s about showing up as a mature product from day one.

![Launch strategy preview](/images/strategy-01.jpg)

## Brand foundation

We define or refine your visual identity so it works across all surfaces.

- Logo design or rework
- Product mockups and visual direction
- Consistent design language

This ensures your product looks cohesive wherever it appears.

## Social and profile setup

Your presence across platforms is structured, not improvised.

- Avatars, covers, and post formats
- Founder profile (LinkedIn) with a clear structure
- Product and personal links with consistent visuals

Everything is aligned with your product and brand.

![Launch strategy preview](/images/strategy-02.jpg)

## Platform focus

Instead of spreading thin, we identify 2–3 core platforms based on:

- Your product type
- Your audience
- Your preferences

This keeps your efforts focused and sustainable.

## Product Hunt launch setup

We prepare everything needed for a clean, high-quality launch.

- Visual assets and thumbnails
- Page structure and positioning
- Recommended content and messaging

So your launch feels deliberate, not rushed.

![Launch strategy preview](/images/strategy-03.jpg)

## A connected presence

The launch strategy ties together:

- Landing page → first impression
- Product → actual experience
- Personal site → identity and context
- Brand store → community layer

Everything reinforces the same narrative.
