The personal site adds a human layer to your product. It introduces the founder, connects your work, and gives context to what you’re building.

It’s not meant to be a content engine. It’s a simple, structured presence that strengthens your brand outside of algorithms.

![Personal site preview](/images/personal-01.jpg)

## A focused founder profile

The personal site acts as a central profile.

- Product links and projects
- Social profiles and contact points
- A clear introduction of who you are

Everything is minimal, intentional, and aligned with your product.

## A few meaningful posts

Instead of constant publishing, we design 2–3 well-structured posts.

- Introduction
- Philosophy or way of thinking
- Optional deep-dive into your product or process

These posts give people context and depth without requiring ongoing content production.

![Personal site preview](/images/personal-02.jpg)

## Built for presence, not algorithms

The goal is not to become a blogger.

It’s to create a space that:

- Represents your thinking
- Adds personality to your product
- Exists independently from social platforms

Something people can return to when they want to understand you.

![Personal site preview](/images/personal-03.jpg)

## Strengthening the ecosystem

The personal site connects back to:

- Your product → what you’re building
- Your documentation → how it works
- Your brand → how it feels

It turns a product into something more complete and relatable.
