Authentication is where your product experience begins. It connects your public-facing site with your internal product, turning visitors into users.

It’s fully integrated into the same repository as your landing page and dashboard, so flows feel consistent from the first click.

![Authentication preview](/images/auth-01.jpg)

## Built-in authentication flows

Sign up, login, and onboarding are already structured and ready to use.

- Email and password authentication
- Social providers (Google, GitHub)
- Clean, minimal onboarding flows

Everything is designed to reduce friction while keeping a polished, trustworthy experience.

## Optional Supabase integration

Authentication can be connected to Supabase out of the box, with a pre-configured structure for users, sessions, and permissions.

You can use it as-is or adapt it to your own backend — the system stays flexible without losing structure.

![Authentication preview](/images/auth-03.jpg)

## Designed as part of the system

Authentication is not an isolated feature. It connects directly to:

- Landing page → user entry point
- Dashboard → authenticated experience
- Shared components and design tokens

![Authentication preview](/images/auth-02.jpg)

This ensures a seamless transition from marketing to product, without visual or structural gaps.
