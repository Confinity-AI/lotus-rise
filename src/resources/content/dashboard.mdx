The dashboard is your product surface. It’s where authenticated users interact with your system, data, and workflows.

It builds directly on top of authentication, with user profiles, session management, and protected routes already in place.

![Dashboard](/images/dashboard-01.jpg)

## Fully integrated user system

User profiles, authenticated navigation, and session handling are connected out of the box.

- Authenticated header with user context
- Profile structure and account management
- Session handling via Supabase integration

Everything is wired together so you don’t have to build the foundation from scratch.

## Structured, ready-to-adapt pages

All dashboard pages are delivered as structured frontend skeletons tailored to your product.

Instead of starting from empty screens, you get a system you can plug your data into:

- Data-heavy dashboards
- Analytics and visualizations
- Social feeds and activity streams
- Product and inventory management

The structure is there — you adapt it to your business logic.

![Dashboard](/images/dashboard-02.jpg)

## Designed around your use case

Each dashboard is shaped based on your product.

Whether you’re building:

- A SaaS product
- A social platform
- An internal tool
- An ecommerce system

The layout, density, and components are designed to match how your product actually works.

## Part of a connected system

The dashboard connects directly to:

- Authentication → user state and access
- Landing page → acquisition and conversion
- Shared components → consistent UI across all surfaces

Everything evolves together as a single system.
