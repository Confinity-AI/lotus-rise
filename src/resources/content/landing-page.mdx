The landing page is the center of your digital presence. It aligns your brand, messaging, and design into a single system built to convert and build trust.

It’s not a standalone page — it’s part of a unified repository that includes authentication and your product dashboard, ensuring consistency across every surface.

## A complete, scalable marketing foundation

From your homepage to blog, pricing, contact, and product pages — everything is structured and connected.

This is not a one-page template. It’s a full marketing system designed to grow with your product.

## MDX-powered content management

All dynamic content is MDX-based, with Once UI components available everywhere.

You can update blog posts, legal pages, and long-form content without touching layout or styling. Content stays consistent, while still allowing flexibility where it matters.
